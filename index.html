The hidden line item

Agency staffing has a cost most homes never see.

Every long-term care home in Ontario depends on agency staff to keep units safe. Few have a reliable way to verify what they are billed for. The result is a quiet, recurring leak: hours that were rounded up, shifts that ran short but invoiced long, timesheets nobody had time to check, and a finance team reconciling paper against spreadsheets weeks after the care was delivered.

Unverified timesheets

Agency hours are self reported, signed at the end of a tired shift, and rarely compared with when the person actually arrived and left.

Rounded and inflated hours

A 7 h 20 min shift billed as 8 hours is a 9% overcharge. Across every shift, every agency, every month, rounding is a budget line of its own.

Manual audit overhead

Director of Care and AP time spent chasing discrepancies, resending invoices and settling disputes. Work that produces no care and no savings.

Software subscription fees

The tools sold to fix this charge the home a licence per seat, per site, per month. The home pays twice: once for the problem and once for the software.

Why pay monthly software fees just to manage staffing?

Staflow costs your home nothing.

No licence, no per user fee, no per site fee, no implementation fee, no contract term. Not a trial and not a tier. The platform is funded by a small per shift fee paid by the staffing agencies that use it to receive your requests and bill you. Every agency invoice you receive is built from clock in and clock out times verified to the minute at your own kiosk.

How it works

Three steps from the shift to the invoice.

The kiosk is the record. The invoice is a report of hours your home already approved.

STEP 1

Clocked in

Agency staff clock in and out on a locked tablet at the unit with a personal PIN. The time is recorded to the minute and tied to the scheduled shift, visible to your home and the agency at the same moment.

STEP 2

Approved

Your Director of Care or scheduler reviews the shift as it happened. Late arrivals and early departures are already on the record. Every correction needs a reason and keeps the original.

STEP 3

Verified

The agency drafts its invoice from the approved shifts only. It cannot add hours that were not clocked. Your team approves it on the same screen and accounts payable receives an invoice that is already matched.

Vendor strategy

You hold the purchasing power. Set the standard.

Long-term care homes decide which agencies they work with, and on what terms. Adding one line to those terms, "shifts are requested, verified and invoiced through Staflow", gives every agency the same rules, the same record and the same proof of hours. Staflow does not replace your vendors or sit between you and them. It is the shared workspace you both use.
