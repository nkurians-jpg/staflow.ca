What you saw at the booth

One platform. Every home, every agency, every shift.

Staflow is built for networks of long-term care homes. Each home runs its own schedule, kiosk and agency relationships. Corporate sees all of it at once, live, without asking anyone for a report.

Facility dashboard

Who is on shift right now, what is unfilled, agency spend this month and the recent timesheets, each marked verified or variance.

Kiosk at the unit

Agency staff select their name and clock in with a PIN. A locked tablet, no paper, no signature at the end of a tired shift. Time is recorded to the minute.

Verified invoice

The agency drafts its invoice from the approved shifts only. Every line traces back to a clock in, a clock out and an approver. Your team approves it on the same screen.

Also included: the agency portal your vendors use to receive requests, assign staff and submit invoices, and mobile apps for facility, agency and staff users.

What it costs your home

Nothing.

No licence, no per user fee, no per site fee, no implementation fee, no contract term. Not a trial and not a tier. The platform is funded by a small per shift fee paid by the staffing agencies that use it to receive your requests and bill you.

Questions we hear at the booth

Do we have to change the agencies we use?

No. Staflow does not replace your vendors or sit between you and them. It is the shared workspace you both use. You keep the agencies you have; they receive your requests, assign staff and invoice through the platform.

Will our agencies agree to it?

You set the terms your agencies work under. Agencies gain faster shift broadcasting, instant assignment and invoices that are approved on review instead of disputed line by line. Staflow onboards and supports each agency directly, so your team does not run a rollout for your vendors.

What hardware do we need?

One tablet per kiosk location, mounted at the unit or the main entrance. It arrives enrolled in mobile device management and locked to the Staflow kiosk only: no browser, no settings. Everyone else uses a normal web browser.

How long does it take to go live?

Days. A home is configured in a single working session: units, roles, rate cards, agencies and the kiosk. Nothing is installed on your servers and nothing has to be integrated before go live.

Where is our data kept?

In Canada, on Google Cloud in the Toronto region, encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups. Access is role based: corporate sees the network, a home sees its own site, an agency sees only its own staff and invoices.

What does it cost the agencies?

A small fee per completed shift, billed monthly. There is nothing to buy up front and nothing to pay for an agency that is not staffing you. Your home pays nothing, ever, and is never affected by an agency's account.
